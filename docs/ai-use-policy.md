# AI use and human review policy

## Scope and inventory

Generative AI may assist with code, copy, educational content, tests, and
documentation. OpenAI Codex is the AI-assisted development tool currently known
to have been used on this project. This inventory must be updated if another AI
tool is introduced.

Syntax Voyager does not include or invoke an AI model at runtime. Adding a
runtime AI feature requires a separate legal, privacy, security, and product
assessment before release, followed by updated public disclosures.

## Publication gate

Before publishing an AI-assisted change, the maintainer must review:

- technical and educational accuracy;
- security, privacy, and accessibility impact;
- third-party rights, licensing, and source provenance;
- test evidence and user-visible disclosures.

The completed review should be recorded in the commit or release notes. This
policy governs changes after its adoption; it does not certify historical review
or legal compliance.
